Kiwi Network

Open, operated, verifiable.

Every merchant on the network publishes a verifiable public projection of what they sell and how to reach them. Buyer and merchant agents talk directly over open protocols. The network helps you discover; it never gets in the way of the deal.

中文：Kiwi Network——开放、受运营、可验证。网络帮你发现；绝不插足交易本身。

Open source. Merchant data stays merchant-controlled.
