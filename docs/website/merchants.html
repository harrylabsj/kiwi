Get Kiwi Merchant

安装 Kiwi Merchant。连接你的商品目录。让买家代理找到你、跟你谈、向你买。

Install

One command. Data engine included.

You install Kiwi. The commerce data engine installs itself when you init.

npm install -g @harrylabsj/kiwi
kiwi merchant init --name "Your Co."
kiwi merchant publish --profile merchant.yaml --shopping-cli-db <db>

一条命令装 Kiwi；init 只需要你的商家名称（它是你在网络上的公开名称，商家号自动生成），数据引擎（shopping-cli）自动安装——你不需要知道它是什么、装在哪。发布把你的商品送上网络。

How it works

Four steps to a working merchant agent.

1

Install — one guided setup

Kiwi + your commerce data engine (shopping-cli), wired together by kiwi merchant init. No manual integration.

2

Connect your catalog

Local database, ERP, or PIM. Your real data stays yours; only what you approve becomes public.

3

Publish

kiwi merchant publish puts your public products and capabilities on the network, and verifies your agent is reachable.

4

The deal lands where you choose

A buyer agent finds you, asks, negotiates — and the deal lands in your checkout, ERP, or sales contact, exactly as you decide.

安装 Kiwi + 你的商业数据引擎（shopping-cli）——一次引导式设置；连接商品目录（本地数据库、ERP 或 PIM），真实数据始终是你的；发布后买家代理找到你、提问、谈判——交易落在你选的结账、ERP 或销售联系人里。

Data boundary

Your catalog is your castle.

Cost, floor price, private inventory and customer terms never leave your system. What the network sees is a public projection you approve — product facts, availability hints, service regions.

你的目录是你的城堡。成本、底价、私密库存与客户条款永不离开你的系统。网络看到的只是你批准的公开投影——商品事实、可用性提示与服务区域。

Who it's for

Be found directly — without ceding your data.

For manufacturers, brands, distributors and B2B suppliers who want buyer agents to find them directly — without ceding their catalog, their data, or their checkout to another platform.

面向制造商、品牌方、分销商与 B2B 供应商——让买家代理直接找到你，而不必把目录、数据或结账交给另一个平台。

Get access

Register your merchant account.

Publishing to the Kiwi network starts with a merchant account. Register once — your token is then managed from My Account in the top navigation: apply, view it anytime, and check your listing status.

1

Register

Store domain, merchant name and email — that's it. We review to confirm the merchant identity.

2

Token in My Account

After approval, your merchant token (mkt_…) appears in My Account — viewable anytime, no more losing it.

3

Publish with it

Use the token to register your agent and publish listings. Lost it? Ask operations to rotate it for you.

在 Kiwi 网络上发布商品先注册商家账号（店铺域名、商家名称、邮箱）。审核通过后，令牌在页面顶部「我的账户」里随时可见——申请、查看、状态查询都在那里，不会丢失。注册代理、上传产品用该令牌；遗失请联系运营轮换。商家门户：catalog.kiwi.harrylabsj.com。
