# Local owner accounting recovery

`LocalMerchantOwnerSession` keeps business evidence and budget accounting separate. Both committed dispatch and SDK turns still use their original authoritative result rules. If budget settlement fails, they throw `OwnerAccountingFailure`, with a fixed `owner_accounting_failed` message, the original business outcome, operation/lease/token binding, the budget error, and any original business/journal errors. An authoritative business success remains `state=settled` in the journal; an unknown business outcome remains reconciliation. The exception is never an ordinary successful submit response and never authorizes business redrive.

New operations carry optional `budget_accounting_state=pending`, changed to `settled` only after successful settlement and bound journal persistence. Pending accounting prevents repeating the same business; existing records without the new field retain their previous semantics. No schema or database migration is introduced.

The only added recovery seam is `session.retryAccounting(failure)` for the trusted local host. Its argument must be the actual failure object issued by that live session, registered privately in a WeakMap. JSON, clones, another session, or a restarted process cannot supply recovery authority. It rechecks the exact operation, merchant, principal, original lease and token amount, full journal digest and current strong grant. A committed operation additionally revalidates the original subject/WebAuthn authority, before and after asynchronous settlement. A repeated successful recovery is idempotent. Recovery performs no new lease acquisition, candidate claim, business effect, provider request or automatic business retry.

A missing/changed journal, revoked/changed grant, expired/revoked committed authority or lost live-session context fails closed for manual accounting reconciliation. The persistent budget reservation is not refunded or changed to zero. The existing real budget gate retains failed settlements as active leases; recovery retries only that original lease. If ledger settlement succeeded but journal confirmation failed, both the typed failure and the private live context retain the monotonic fact `budgetSettled=true`. A later permitted recovery retries only the journal confirmation and never calls settlement again. An initial budget failure still retries the original lease through the underlying idempotent accounting.

Diagnostics are local objects for the trusted host; their original errors must not be automatically serialized into a public API or logged with grant, model, key or database content. This adds no CLI/HTTP route and does not change owner switches, provider guards, WeakMap committed permits or authoritative receipt checks.

The legacy `checkFileGrant` compatibility helper now delegates to `createFileGrantResolver().readStrong()`. It retains the legacy principal-mismatch mapping to `grant_merchant_mismatch`, preserves exact opaque IDs and optional missing `granted_at`, and rejects whitespace identities or invalid present `granted_at`. The current resolver's canonical digest and strong rereads remain unchanged.
